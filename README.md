🔥 Arduino-Based Fire Fighting Robot

---

📌 Project Overview

---

The Fire Fighting Robot is an Arduino-based automated robotic system designed to detect and extinguish fire with minimal human intervention. It uses flame sensors to identify the direction of fire and moves towards the detected source. Once the fire is detected, a water pump is activated to spray water and extinguish the flames.

The robot also integrates a SIM800L GSM module to send SMS alerts and make emergency calls, providing real-time notifications.

⚙️ Components Used

- Arduino UNO R3
- L293D Motor Driver Shield
- Flame Sensors
- Gas Sensor
- SIM800L GSM Module
- Servo Motor
- Buck Converter
- DC Motors
- Water Pump

🚀 Working Principle

1. Flame sensors detect the presence and direction of fire.
2. The Arduino processes the sensor readings.
3. The robot moves towards the fire source.
4. It stops near the fire and activates the water pump.
5. The servo motor helps direct the water spray.
6. The SIM800L module sends an SMS alert and makes an emergency call.

✨ Key Features

- Automatic fire detection
- Fire direction identification
- Autonomous movement towards fire
- Automatic water spraying
- GSM-based SMS and call alerts
- Reduced human intervention during fire emergencies

🎯 Applications

- Homes and buildings
- Industrial environments
- Laboratories
- Fire-prone areas
